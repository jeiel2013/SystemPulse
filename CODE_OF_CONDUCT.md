# Code of conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [Português Brasileiro](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.pt-BR.md)

Treat other contributors with respect. Discuss code and evidence, not personal
traits. Be welcoming to people with different experience levels and backgrounds.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and publishing another person's
private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
needed. To report conduct privately, use the repository's private maintainer
contact if available; otherwise open an issue requesting a private channel
without posting the incident details. Reports will be handled with discretion
and with attention to the safety of the people involved.
